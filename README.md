# ScamShield Bharat

### Pause. Verify. Stay Safe.

ScamShield Bharat is an investor-safety web application designed to help users identify potential warning signs in suspicious financial and investment-related messages before they act.

## Problem

Digital financial scams can reach users through messages, social media, unrealistic return promises, urgent payment requests, and requests for sensitive information.

First-time investors and users with limited financial or digital literacy may find it difficult to recognize these warning signs.

## Solution

ScamShield Bharat provides a simple safety layer where users can paste a suspicious message and analyze it for potential risk indicators.

The application:
- Identifies potential warning signs
- Explains why each indicator may be risky
- Provides safer next steps
- Supports English and Hindi
- Follows a privacy-first approach

## Trust & Guardrails

ScamShield Bharat does NOT:
- Provide Buy/Sell/Hold recommendations
- Predict stock prices or investment returns
- Promote financial products
- Ask users for OTPs, passwords, or UPI PINs

The application highlights potential warning signs and encourages users to independently verify information through official sources.

## Target Users

- First-time investors
- Retail investors
- Young users exposed to financial content on social media
- Tier-2 and Tier-3 users
- Users with limited digital or financial literacy
